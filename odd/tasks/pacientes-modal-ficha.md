# Pacientes: la ficha completa en un modal

Objective: opening a patient from /pacientes shows the full record in a MorphDialog with the reference design.
Branch: working tree of `feat/HU-021-lista-turnos`, no commits until Leo asks. Delivery: single-pr (later). Runner: vitest + tsc.

## Specs
- S1: "que el modal que se abra tenga la info de la pgiana de la ficha completa, algo asi com ose ve la imagen" — tapping a patient opens the full record (same content as /pacientes/[id]) inside a MorphDialog growing from the card.
- S2: Intercepted route: URL becomes /pacientes/<id>; reload or a shared link renders the full page; Back closes the modal; navigating elsewhere closes it.
- S3: Single `PatientRecord` component used by the page and the modal (no duplicated loading or markup).
- S4: Header like the reference: big initials avatar, name, sex, age, birth date, stage pill top right, buttons "Agendar cita", "Editar perfil", "Archivar" (our "Dar de baja"), "Ver historial". Reuses existing actions.
- S5: Underlined tabs in the reference style, keeping our tabs (Resumen, Consultas, Planes, Historial). The full page gets the same design (Leo: "Sí, con este plan").

- S6: "Quiero implementar esta animación en pacientes, cuando se abre y cierra modal, ya tenemos algo, pero quiero que lo mejoremos hasta llegar al nivel del video" — row lifts into a white card (~60 ms), avatar/name/stage travel and scale into the header staying sharp, body reveals top to bottom while unblurring, close reaches the row in ~170 ms. Other MorphDialogs unchanged (opt-in).
